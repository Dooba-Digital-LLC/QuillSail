# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Be constructive when reviewing work, make room for different levels of experience, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, subscriber data, private messages, or exploit details in public discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community. Apply these standards fairly, explain moderation decisions where practical, and handle reports confidentially.

Report conduct concerns to repository maintainers through a private contact method on their GitHub profiles. Do not publish private evidence in an issue. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
